Core Platform

Everything your restaurant needs to thrive

From table-side QR ordering to real-time kitchen routing and GST-compliant exports, RestaurantOS handles every aspect of your restaurant operations.

QR Code Ordering

Auto-generate unique QR codes for each table. Customers scan, browse the digital menu, and place orders directly from their phones — no waiters needed.

Kitchen Display (KDS)

Orders land instantly on your kitchen screen with real-time status tracking — received, preparing, ready. No paper tickets, no confusion.

GST-Ready Billing

Auto-calculate CGST/SGST based on your state code. Export CA-ready tax reports with HSN codes for seamless compliance filing.

Owner Analytics

Real-time dashboard with daily sales, table occupancy, order volume, 7-day revenue charts, and recent transaction history — all at a glance.

Table Management

Visual table grid with occupancy indicators. Start seatings, assign waiters, and monitor capacity in real-time with drag-and-drop ease.

Staff & Role Access

Create owner, kitchen, and waiter accounts with role-based access control. Waiters see only their assigned tables — fully scoped permissions.

Getting Started

Up and running in 4 simple steps

No complex installations, no expensive hardware. Just configure, customize, and launch your digital restaurant.

1

Create Account

Sign up and connect your Firebase project. RestaurantOS provisions your secure cloud database in seconds.

2

Configure Menu

Add your categories and dishes via the visual menu builder. Set prices, tags, and optional dish images.

3

Setup Tables

Define your seating layout and generate unique QR codes for each table. Print and place on table stands.

4

Go Live!

Customers scan QR codes to order. Orders flow to your KDS. Billing prints instantly. You're digital.
